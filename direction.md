# Index direction

The homepage is a quiet, dark index of Boston Rohan's current life and work.

- Large identity first; no conventional hero card, eyebrow, or button cluster.
- A single daily record follows the identity, combining whatever safe live signals are available into one composed moment.
- Work and experience are visible on the first page as ordered lists with dates/status, not repeated cards.
- Life is represented through music, anime, Mom, Mikey, Grandma, and Sasha, with future photos/artifacts able to interrupt the clean structure.
- Journal influence appears through occasional handwriting, scans, and authored artifacts—not through a fake notebook UI or a handwriting font for body copy.
- Palette: near-black, warm off-white, muted gray, quiet blue links, and restrained orange date accents.
- Grammar: hairlines, generous negative space, oversized type, small data typography, sharp alignment, no rounded card/button system, no gradients, no full-white light mode.
- Motion should be limited to one authored page-entry/reveal moment and meaningful live-signal transitions.
